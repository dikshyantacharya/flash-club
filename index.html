Table activity
    The house rules 100 chips · 2 players · 3 cards

    Each player starts with 100 play chips. Both pay a 5-chip ante each hand. Blind bets cost 5 chips per unit; seen bets cost twice as much. Reveal only on your turn. Bets track units, so revealing changes the cost of your remaining call, not previously paid chips. One raise adds one unit. Raises are limited to what both players can afford. Check only when no bet is outstanding; two checks or a call ends the hand. Folding awards the pot to your rival. Ties split the pot. First seat alternates each hand. When a player runs out of chips, both must agree to restart.

    High to low: three of a kind, straight flush, straight, flush, pair, high card. A-K-Q is the highest straight, A-2-3 second; suits never break ties. This is a compact heads-up house variant of Teen Patti.
