    The house rules 100 chips · 2 players · 3 cards

    Each player starts with 100 play chips. Both pay a 5-chip ante each hand. Blind bets cost 5 chips per unit; seen bets cost twice as much. Reveal only on your turn. Bets track units, so revealing changes the cost of your remaining call, not previously paid chips. One raise adds one unit. Raises are limited to what both players can afford. Check only when no bet is outstanding; two consecutive checks reveal both hands immediately. Calling matches the bet and keeps the hand going; either player can raise again or check. Folding awards the pot to your rival. Ties split the pot. First seat alternates each hand. After each result, both choose Next hand to continue with their balances. When a player has fewer than 5 chips, both can restart with 100 each.

    High to low: three of a kind, straight flush, straight, flush, pair, high card. A-K-Q is the highest straight, A-2-3 second; suits never break ties. This is a compact heads-up house variant of Teen Patti.

    Keep both tabs open. Leaving or disconnecting ends the session. Room passwords control entry, not user identity. The host deals and validates moves; play with friends you trust. No purchases, deposits, or cash payouts.
