    The house rules 100 chips · 2 players · 3 cards

    Each player starts with 100 play chips. Both pay a 5-chip ante each hand. The blind stake starts at 5 chips. On every turn, call the current stake (double if you have seen your cards), raise, show, or fold. There is no Check. One raise increases the blind stake by 5; a seen player pays twice that stake. Calling pays a fresh wager and keeps the hand going. If either player is blind, only a blind player can Show. Once both have seen their cards, either can Show on their turn. Show pays the current call plus a flat 5 and immediately reveals both hands. Raises are limited to what both players can afford. An all-in wager gets a response, then cards reveal automatically; if an ante uses a player's last chips, cards reveal immediately. Seeing only your own cards is free. Folding awards the pot to your rival. Ties split the pot. First seat alternates each hand. After each result, both choose Next hand to continue with their balances. When a player has fewer than 5 chips, both can restart with 100 each.

    High to low: three of a kind, straight flush, straight, flush, pair, high card. A-K-Q is the highest straight, A-2-3 second; suits never break ties. This is a compact heads-up house variant of Teen Patti.

    Keep both tabs open. Leaving or disconnecting ends the session. Room passwords control entry, not user identity. The host deals and validates moves; play with friends you trust. No purchases, deposits, or cash payouts.
